# Claim-to-evidence ledger

This ledger covers empirical and source-based claims in [PAPER.md](../PAPER.md). Metrics are for the included synthetic fixtures; they do not measure detection of live personal data or agent task outcomes.

| ID | Claim | Evidence class | Evidence | Caveat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M1** | On 101 labeled cases (13 Stripe fake card format markers, 3 RFC 9542 documentation-range MAC markers, and 85 negative controls), deterministic aggregate precision was 0.8889 and recall was 0.5000 (8 TP, 1 FP, 8 FN). The simple regex baseline had precision 0.2581 and recall 1.0000 (16 TP, 46 FP, 0 FN). | `measured` | `benchmark/metrics/summary.json`; `benchmark/metrics/trial-metrics.json`; generated at the timestamp in `benchmark/run-manifest.json`. | Format-marker labels, not actual PII truth. | **M2** | Deterministic per-category results were card 5 TP/0 FP/8 FN (precision 1.0000, recall 0.3846), MAC 3 TP/1 FP/0 FN (precision 0.7500, recall 1.0000), and zero false positives for the four negative-only categories. The baseline false-positive counts were 10 card, 8 SSN, 8 email, 15 IPv4, 2 IPv6, and 3 MAC. | `measured` | Same metric files as M1. | Negative-only categories do not establish positive detection ability or recall. |
